SOUND EFFECT CREDITS (public/sfx)
================================

Every sample in this folder is CC0 1.0 (Creative Commons Zero / public domain
dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is
required; it is given here anyway.

Sources
-------
* Kenney (https://www.kenney.nl) - three packs, all CC0:
    - "Impact Sounds"    (version 1.0, 2019-12-19)
    - "Interface Sounds"
    - "Music Jingles"
  Support Kenney: https://www.patreon.com/kenney/  and  https://kenney.nl/support
* rubberduck - pack "sfx_breaking_and_falling" (75 sounds, "bfh1_*" files), published
  on OpenGameArt.org under CC0 1.0.

The files were copied as-is (Ogg Vorbis, not re-encoded or edited); only the names changed.
Per-file playback loudness normalisation lives in src/sfx.js (MANIFEST gains).
